# Revenue Model

## Revenue streams
### Affiliate
Metrics:
- clicks
- conversion rate
- EPC
- commission per order
- refund/cancellation rate
- revenue per 1,000 views
- contribution margin per content item

### Sponsorship
Metrics:
- qualified leads
- proposal rate
- close rate
- average contract value
- campaign margin
- payment delay
- repeat rate

### Services
Examples:
- short-form content packages
- UGC production
- social management
- affiliate campaign management
- creative testing
- performance reporting

Metrics:
- billable revenue
- delivery cost
- gross margin
- utilization
- customer retention
- accounts receivable days

## Unit economics
For each channel/content/creator:
Revenue - variable production cost - attributable distribution cost = contribution margin.

Do not use views or followers as economic substitutes.

## Treasury policy
Cash is segmented into:
- operating reserve
- payroll reserve
- committed obligations
- experiment budget
- growth budget.

No agent may directly edit balances. Money changes only through validated ledger transactions.

## Distress policy
WARNING -> COST CONTROL -> DISTRESS -> EMERGENCY -> LIQUIDATION -> BANKRUPTCY.
At each state, discretionary spending authority is reduced.
