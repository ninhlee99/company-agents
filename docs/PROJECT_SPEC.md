# Autonomous Company OS — Project Specification

## Mission
Build an AI-native company operating system in which autonomous agents operate an economically grounded organization with capital, revenue, expenses, people, customers, products, experiments, and business units.

## Primary objective
Maximize long-term, risk-adjusted sustainable free cash flow while maintaining solvency and obeying the Company Constitution.

## Initial business
AI-native media and creator company. The media vertical is the first proving ground, not the final product.

## Core capabilities
1. Company state and organizational structure
2. Immutable double-entry accounting
3. Budgets and capital allocation
4. Agent orchestration and permissions
5. Governance and approvals
6. Hiring and workforce management
7. Customer and sales operations
8. Creator/content operations
9. Experiments and learning
10. Business-unit creation, scaling and shutdown
11. Distress and bankruptcy
12. Simulation before real-world autonomy

## Product principles
- Deterministic code is the source of truth for money, permissions and state transitions.
- LLMs reason, plan, generate and interpret; they do not directly mutate critical state.
- Every material action is traceable to an agent, decision, authorization and outcome.
- All external providers are adapters behind interfaces.
- The system must remain runnable after every milestone.

## Initial success test
A simulated company starts with finite capital and must discover profitable activities, allocate capital, control costs, hire when justified, scale winners, stop losers and either survive or become bankrupt according to its decisions.

## Non-goals
- unrestricted financial autonomy
- bypassing laws or platform rules
- fabricated customers/revenue
- hidden spending
- self-modification of governance or permissions
