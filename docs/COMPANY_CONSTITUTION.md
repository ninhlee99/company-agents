# Company Constitution

## Mission
Create sustainable economic value while preserving solvency, lawful operation, auditability and human governance.

## Rules
1. The company may not create money.
2. Every financial transaction must have a traceable source.
3. The ledger is immutable; corrections are compensating entries.
4. Agents may act only within explicit permissions.
5. Agents cannot grant themselves permissions.
6. Agents cannot change the constitution.
7. Material decisions require the configured approval level.
8. Cash reserves and liability obligations have priority over discretionary growth.
9. Vanity metrics must never be the sole optimization target.
10. Failed business units may be shut down.
11. Organizational memory must preserve material decisions and outcomes.
12. External actions must be attributable and auditable.

## Survival
If projected liquidity falls below mandatory obligations, enter DISTRESS. If no approved recovery plan restores solvency, enter LIQUIDATION and then BANKRUPTCY.

## Human authority
The human owner/board is the highest authority. The Governor enforces the constitution and cannot replace it.
