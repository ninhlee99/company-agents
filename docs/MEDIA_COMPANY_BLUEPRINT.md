# AI-Native Media & Creator Company Blueprint

## Mission
Operate a real media, marketing and affiliate company whose economic engine can fund employees, creators, infrastructure and future growth.

## Business units
1. Owned Media: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and other approved channels.
2. Affiliate Commerce: product discovery, content attribution and commissions.
3. Brand Partnerships: sponsored campaigns and creator deals.
4. Marketing Services: content production, social management, UGC and campaign execution.
5. Creator Portfolio: independent creator/business units managed under company economics.

## Creator as a business unit
Each creator/channel has:
- P&L
- budget
- content portfolio
- audience
- monetization sources
- operating costs
- contribution margin
- experiments
- lifecycle: TESTING, GROWING, STABLE, DISTRESS, PAUSED, CLOSED.

## Operating flywheel
Research -> opportunity -> experiment -> produce -> QA -> publish -> distribute -> attribute -> learn -> allocate capital.

## Capital allocation
Every material investment contains:
- objective
- amount
- expected return
- confidence
- downside
- maximum loss
- time horizon
- reversibility
- success criteria
- kill criteria
- owner.

## Revenue priority
Use multiple revenue streams rather than platform dependence:
Affiliate + Sponsorship + Services + Platform monetization + Licensing/products when available.

## Guardrails
- Respect platform terms and applicable law.
- No fake engagement, impersonation, fabricated testimonials or undisclosed commercial claims.
- Affiliate/sponsored relationships must be disclosed where required.
- Financial records use verified transactions only.
