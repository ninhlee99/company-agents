# Veridara AI — Company Charter

## Identity

**Veridara AI** is an AI-native operating company.

Its internal workforce is a network of specialized AI agents coordinated by the **Autonomous Company OS**. The OS is the company's operating infrastructure, not the company's public identity.

> **Veridara AI builds, operates and compounds AI-native businesses.**

**Tagline:** Build. Operate. Learn. Compound.

## What the company does

Veridara AI combines AI agents for strategy, finance, operations, growth, content and analysis with durable company memory, deterministic governance, double-entry accounting, commercial CRM, proposals, sponsorships, invoicing, media production and affiliate-commerce infrastructure.

The purpose is to turn opportunities into controlled experiments, commercial transactions and measurable economic outcomes.

## Initial business lines

- **Media & Creator Commerce** — produce commercially useful media and connect content to monetization.
- **Affiliate Commerce** — discover products through approved adapters, track attribution and reconcile verified commissions.
- **B2B AI Services** — package AI capabilities into proposals, commercial engagements and recurring client relationships.
- **Commercial Partnerships** — manage proposals, sponsorships, invoices and receivables with durable state and accounting.
- **Future Venture Portfolio** — create additional AI-native business units when evidence supports capital allocation.

Agents are not granted unrestricted authority. Reasoning is separated from authorization and execution.

## Economic flywheel

`Signal → Research → Opportunity → Experiment → Customer → Delivery → Revenue → Reconciliation → Learning → Reinvestment`

The goal is not simply to generate AI output. The goal is to create repeatable economic loops with evidence.

## Operating principles

1. **Truth over appearance** — never describe a contract as a completed external action.
2. **Evidence before autonomy** — increase autonomy only after measurable outcomes exist.
3. **Money is deterministic** — accounting and financial invariants do not depend on LLM judgment.
4. **Least privilege** — agents receive only capabilities required for their roles.
5. **Material actions stay gated** — external publishing, messaging, payments and other consequential side effects require explicit integration and approval controls.
6. **Everything important is durable** — critical decisions and commercial state belong in the database and audit trail.
7. **Learn from outcomes** — successful experiments should improve future allocation; failed experiments should reduce repeated waste.

## Current boundary

Veridara AI has a strong internal operating foundation, but it is not yet a fully autonomous external company.

Explicit acceptance gates remain for:

- real social-platform publishing;
- production outbound email/messaging;
- payment-rail initiation and provider reconciliation;
- multi-user identity/RBAC/SSO;
- multi-tenant SaaS isolation;
- evidence-backed autonomous portfolio allocation;
- production model evaluation and routing.

The company should currently be described as **controlled autonomy infrastructure with commercial capabilities**, until at least one end-to-end revenue loop is proven with real external delivery, real payment settlement and reconciliation.

## Long-term vision

Build a company in which human leadership defines mission, risk appetite and constitutional constraints while AI agents continuously discover opportunities, research markets, propose strategy, create and deliver products/services, manage customers, measure unit economics, learn from outcomes and scale validated business units.

The intended outcome is not “AI that talks like a company”.

It is **a company whose operating system is built around AI**.
