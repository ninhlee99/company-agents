# Company Description

**Company Agents** is an autonomous company operating system.

It treats a company as an economic system rather than a collection of chatbots. AI agents receive explicit roles, skills, budgets and permissions. They observe company state, propose actions, obtain authorization, execute through deterministic tools, measure outcomes and update organizational memory.

The first business vertical is an AI-native media and creator factory. The long-term platform is vertical-agnostic: the same Company OS can operate services, SaaS, media, commerce or holding-company business units.

Core idea: agents must create measurable economic value or the company eventually runs out of capital.
