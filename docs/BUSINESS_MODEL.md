# Business Model

## Initial vertical: AI Media & Creator Factory

The company runs many small content experiments and scales formats with positive economics.

### Revenue
- platform monetization where eligible
- sponsorships
- affiliate/commerce
- memberships/subscriptions
- licensing/IP
- creator management revenue share
- B2B branded content
- digital products and services

### Content flywheel
Research → idea → script → production → publish → measure → attribute revenue → learn → reinvest.

### Creator types
- AI-native creator
- faceless creator
- human creator
- hybrid creator

### Unit economics
Track revenue, production cost, distribution cost, talent cost, acquisition cost, contribution margin, revenue per content, revenue per creator and cash payback.

### Expansion
Once repeatable media economics exist, the company may create adjacent businesses such as creator tools, B2B content services, SaaS products or creator management.

## Capital allocation
Every business unit has a budget, thesis, expected return, maximum loss, time horizon and kill criteria.
